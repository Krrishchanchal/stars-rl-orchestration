## Source-parameter inventory

Paper-derived starting inputs: 60 satellites, 4 planes, 500 km altitude, 88-degree inclination; 30 users, 6 slices; GS coordinates (49.93 N, 79.53 W) and (49.93 N, 69.53 W); 10-degree elevation; 14 x 60-second windows; 2–4 functions per chain; 35–150 ms delay limits; 2–20 Mbps throughput; 1000–2000 MIPS satellite capacity; maximum 5 access associations; four ISLs where geometrically valid; 5–90 instructions/bit; reported base processing range 1–17 with units requiring interpretation; 14 GHz, 20 dBm, 15/40 dBi user/satellite gains, 290 K.

Store distributions, units and actual seeded draws. Reported ISL bandwidth does not alone establish that aggregate contention is enforced by the paper's optimization equations.

The specification explicitly selects 12000-bit packets and 100 MHz bandwidth, along with the other defaults in experiment_contract.md, as implementation assumptions. These are not recovered author values. Do not tune geometry to manufacture the reported average visible-satellite count; the regular four-plane geometry has an independent visibility bound that requires investigating the source claim.
